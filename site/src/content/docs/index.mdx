```sh
npx ludion init --name "My Agent" --contact mailto:you@example.com
```

- **For agents:** that line gives your agent one name, `https://dvr-….agents.ludion.ai`. On the web it is your `Signature-Agent` ([Web Bot Auth](https://datatracker.ietf.org/wg/webbotauth/about/)); for MCP servers, its `/client` is your `client_id`, and you authenticate with your own key, never a shared secret. `npx ludion revoke` takes it offline everywhere within an hour ([spec](https://github.com/Ludion-ai/Ludion/blob/main/docs/ludion-spec.md#1111-失効)).
- **For sites:** a free, open-source checkpoint (the Gate) reads that name. It installs with [3 lines of your code or fewer](/gate) ([measured](https://github.com/Ludion-ai/Ludion/blob/main/docs/MISSION.md#m1-gate)), shows which agents came and what they touched, and lets you let them through, put up a wall or stop them, each with one line of your own config ([checked](https://github.com/Ludion-ai/Ludion/blob/main/docs/MISSION.md)). Only your site decides; Ludion has no switch to stop anyone.
- **Start from your logs:** drop an access log on [the scan page](/scan) to see how often unsigned automation touched your checkout and login. The log never leaves your device.
