**HTTP 403** · `Ludion-Error: mandate_required`

## What happened

Your signature verified, but this route does something that needs permission (a purchase, an account change, a booking) and asks for a *Mandate*: a signed, short-lived statement of what you may do on this site. In Ludion Protocol v0 it is the limit your operator puts on you with its Root key; a running agent holds only its session key, so it cannot widen its own. Your request carried none that holds here.

A Mandate that is there counts as none when:

- it is for another site, or for a category this site is not in;
- it has expired;
- it has been withdrawn;
- no `Ludion-Staple` came with it. Whom a Mandate delegates to is checked against the Diver your Staple names.

A Mandate says for whom (`self` when your operator issued it, or a pseudonym unique to each site when a person consented), where, what you may do (`read`, `account`, `post`, `reserve`, `checkout`, `delete`), up to what limit, and until when. It contains no name, address or contact details.

## Why this site asks

Even a legitimate agent can be steered by a prompt injection, and a key can be stolen. A Mandate lets the site check that this action is within what was actually allowed, before it happens: a hijacked agent, or someone signing with its stolen session key, still cannot step outside it.

## How to fix it

- If you run the agent, put a Mandate on it for this site with your Root key (the Registry takes no other), then attach it with the line the command prints:

  ```sh
  npx ludion-ai mandate create --site https://shop.example --scope read
  ```

  Use the site's own origin, and the scopes this route asks for. `--checkout-max`, `--currency` and `--per-day` limit checkouts. Your agent sends the Mandate in the `Ludion-Mandate` header, inside the signature.
- Always send your Staple with a Mandate, with `Ludion-Staple` inside the same signature.
- An expired or withdrawn Mandate stays unusable: create a new one.
- In v0 a Mandate is your operator's own limit, not the consent of the person you act for. That consent page is not open yet.
